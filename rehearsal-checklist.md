# Speech Rehearsal Worksheet

**Goal:** Plan a talk that fits its assigned slot and explain how your measured pace changed your word budget.

## Plan your slot

- [ ] Record the total slot: ____ min ____ sec.
- [ ] Confirm whether Q&A and introductions share the slot. Set aside ____ sec for them if they do.
- [ ] Set aside ____ sec for silent slide inspection, transitions, or demonstrations performed in silence.
- [ ] Subtract those reservations from the total. Time available for spoken delivery: ____ sec.

## Measure and revise

- [ ] Choose an excerpt that sounds like the rest of your talk. Count its words: ____.
- [ ] Deliver it aloud at a comfortable pace. Keep your usual pauses. Record elapsed time: ____ sec.
- [ ] Calculate your pace: words spoken × 60 ÷ elapsed seconds = ____ WPM.
- [ ] Calculate the draft budget: round down available speaking seconds × WPM ÷ 60 = ____ words.
- [ ] Compare the budget with the draft. If the draft is too long, shorten a repeated example or tighten the opening.
- [ ] Rehearse the full talk with its slides and extra pauses. Record the observed duration: ____ sec. Add any in-slot activity excluded from the rehearsal and check the full slot again.

## Try an example

A five-minute slot has 300 seconds. Reserve 60 seconds for questions and 20 seconds for a silent demonstration, leaving 220 seconds. If a rehearsal excerpt of 130 words took 60 seconds, the pace is 130 WPM. The draft budget is floor(220 × 130 ÷ 60) = 476 words.

In class, compare budgets from two speakers rehearsing the same excerpt. Discuss how their measured pace affected the budget and what each would cut if the time limit were shortened. Assessment can use the completed timing record and a full rehearsal.
